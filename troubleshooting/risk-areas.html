⚠ Risk areas — the consolidated support checklist

Every pulsing ⚠ marker from every diagram on this site, in one place: what can go wrong, the symptom, a detection query, and the first response. High severity first. Use it as a health-check template for topology reviews and as the index during incidents.

✅ How to use this page

Quarterly: run every detection query against your topology and record the results — most replication disasters announce themselves weeks earlier as one of these items. During incidents: match the symptom column, jump to the first response. The same content pops up when you click any ⚠ marker on the component diagrams.

Topology review extras (not tied to one component)

  • ☐ Replication creation scripts exist, current, in source control (scripting)
  • ☐ Distribution DB + msdb in backup schedules; restore of the distributor rehearsed on paper
  • ☐ Agent accounts documented with owners; rotation runbook tested (runbook)
  • ☐ Alert chain fire-drilled this quarter (alerts)
  • ☐ Reinitialization impact (time + app outage) estimated per subscription before you need it
  • ☐ Known subscriber-side customizations (indexes, triggers, permissions) scripted for post-reinit replay
